How it's worked out Six sensory axes from the WCR Sensory Lexicon and SCA cupping. Each ingredient is scored 0–5 per 30 ml (estimates), averaged by parts, so water and ice dilute. Milk lowers bitterness, acidity and roast (Keast 2008, Akiyama 2009, Cleve 2025); sugar lowers bitterness (Calviño 1990). Cold brew reads less bitter and acidic than hot (Batali 2022).
